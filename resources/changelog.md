# OpenMyFront

A self-hosted, de-branded fork of OpenFront, set up for private play with friends.

- Play **Single Player** against bots, or **Create Lobby** and share the join link.
- No accounts, store, ads, or external tracking.

## October 2026: synced with OpenFront

Caught up with ~4 months of upstream OpenFront development:

- **40 new maps** (135 total), including Benelux, France, Vietnam, New Zealand,
  Madagascar, Central America, Sol (the Solar System) and Halloween maps.
- **Smarter nations**: better economy, boat defense, nuke targeting, betrayal
  and retaliation logic.
- **Gameplay**: impassable terrain, SAM range scaling on upgrade, trade and rail
  economy pacing, annexing land a nuke cuts off, and many balance fixes.
- **Spectating**: join any lobby to watch without playing.
- **Replay viewer** for finished games.
- **In-game tutorial** for new players.
- **Lobby browser** (Detailed View), player limits for private lobbies, and
  start-time / player-cap options when listing a lobby.
- **Inventory**: pick skins, flags, crowns and effects (all free), with
  numbered loadouts and in-game effect previews.
- **New audio**: menu and gameplay music, new sound effects and ambiences.
- **Settings overhaul**: graphics presets, a Graphics tab, map layers, HUD
  opacity, custom keybinds, snap build preview, Halloween theme toggle.
- Reworked WebGL renderer with lighting, fallout effects and GPU-crash recovery.
